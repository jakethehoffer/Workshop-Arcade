Workshop Arcade

How to Play

Watch the four pads flash in sequence, then repeat the sequence by tapping the pads in the same order. Each successful round adds one new step.

  • Sound is part of the game — each pad has its own tone.
  • Get one step wrong and the run ends.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the pads, then Enter/Space. Or 1-4 for direct pad selection.
  • Press F for fullscreen when the board is focused.
  • Press Esc to close this dialog.
