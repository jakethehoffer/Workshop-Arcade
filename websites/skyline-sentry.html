Skyline Sentry Controls

Defend three lanes until all five waves are clear. Core hits end the run; shield pulses buy space near the core.

  • Arrow Up/Down or W/S: choose lane
  • Space or Enter: fire sentry shot
  • E: pulse shield
  • R: restart, F: fullscreen, ?: help
