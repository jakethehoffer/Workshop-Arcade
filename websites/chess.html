    ❓ How to Play Chess

    Objective: Checkmate your opponent's king by putting it in a position where it cannot escape capture!

    Controls:

    • Click a piece to select it and see its legal moves
    • Click a highlighted square to move the piece there
    • Undo - Take back the last move
    • Restart Game - Start a new game
    • Flip Board - Rotate the board view

    Gameplay:

    • White moves first, then players alternate turns
    • Each piece moves in a specific way (see piece movement rules)
    • You can play against another player or against the computer
    • Adjust the AI depth to change difficulty (higher = stronger but slower)
    • Checkmate occurs when the king is in check and cannot escape
    • Stalemate occurs when a player has no legal moves but is not in check (draw)
