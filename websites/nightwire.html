Stay out of amber cones. Take guards down from behind — but a body in a cone re-trips the alarm. Grab the asset, reach the exit, stay unseen. Each sighting adds 34 to a 100 alarm, so the third one catches you. Standing in a cone is seen twice in one turn, once on the step and again after the guards move. At 50 they go alert and every cone reaches 4 tiles instead of 3. A quiet turn pays back 4.
