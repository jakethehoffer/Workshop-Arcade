Shard Sheriff

Your shot ricochets off the four walls. Land it on every gold badge and never let it reach the blue glass. The dashed sight line shows where the barrel points as far as the first bank, so read the angle rather than guessing. Every wall you bank off before a hit scores more.

Left/Right or Up/Down swings the barrel, and you can tap the board to point it there. Space or Enter fires. S spends one shard on a long sight that reveals the whole path. R restarts, M toggles sound, F toggles fullscreen, and ? opens help.
