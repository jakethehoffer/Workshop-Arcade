❓ How to Play Snake

Objective: Control the snake to eat food and grow as long as possible. Avoid hitting the walls or your own tail!

← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D Move the snake
Space Start game / Pause / Restart after game over
M Toggle music

Tips:

  • Each food eaten increases your score and makes the snake longer
  • Plan your path to avoid trapping yourself
  • On mobile, use the on-screen buttons or swipe on the canvas
