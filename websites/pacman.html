❓ How to Play Pac-Man

Objective: Eat all the dots in the maze while avoiding the ghosts. Collect power pellets to turn the tables!

← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D Move Pac-Man
Pause Button Pause / Resume the game
Reset Button Start a new game

Gameplay:

  • Eat all the small dots to complete the level
  • Collect the large power pellets to make ghosts vulnerable
  • When ghosts are blue, you can eat them for bonus points
  • Avoid touching ghosts when they're not vulnerable
  • You have 3 lives - lose them all and it's game over!
