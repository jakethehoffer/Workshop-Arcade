Racing Controls

Thread the neon loop for three laps.

Drive through each checkpoint in order. Clean lines keep speed; grass and rails slow the car.

  • Arrow keys or W/A/S/D steer and drive.
  • Space spends boost while accelerating.
  • F toggles fullscreen.
  • R restarts the race.
  • The highlighted gate is always the next checkpoint.
