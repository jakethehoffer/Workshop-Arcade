How to play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in up to six tries. Each guess must be a real 5-letter word.

  • Green = right letter, right spot.
  • Yellow = in the word, wrong spot.
  • Slate = not in the word.

Hard mode forces you to reuse revealed letters in the same spots on later guesses.
