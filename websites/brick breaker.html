Power-ups

Multi-Ball — splits the ball into three.
Gun — auto-fires twin bullets for ~5s.

Curses

Shrink — temporarily shrinks the paddle. Raises RISK.
Fast — speeds up gameplay. Raises RISK.

Combo — Hit 2+ bricks in succession within 2s to start a combo.

Risk Multiplier — Curses increase a RISK multiplier that multiplies all points while active.
