How to Play

Hit each falling note as it crosses the pulse line. Cleaner timing earns more points and keeps your combo alive.

  • Keyboard lanes: D, F, J, K.
  • Touch: tap the four lane buttons below the stage.
  • Perfect is within 70ms; Good within 115ms; OK within 165ms.
  • Space or Enter starts or restarts. Esc closes this dialog.
