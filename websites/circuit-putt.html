Mini Golf Controls

Bank the circuit in the fewest strokes.

Drag away from the ball and release to putt. The cue points along the shot line.

  • Arrow keys adjust keyboard aim and power.
  • Space putts when the ball is stopped.
  • R resets the current hole.
  • Sand slows the ball; bumpers and rails add feedback.
