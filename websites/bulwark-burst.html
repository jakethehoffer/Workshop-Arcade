Bulwark Burst Controls

Protect the central core. Drones fly in from the edge, shots build heat, and a shield pulse knocks back close threats on a long cooldown.

  • Arrow Left/Right or A/D: rotate cannon
  • Space or Enter: fire a burst
  • Shift or E: pulse shield
  • Pointer/touch canvas: aim and fire
  • R: restart, F: fullscreen, ?: help
