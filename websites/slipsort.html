How to play Slipsort

  • Arrows / WASD move the gap — the tile beside it slips in. Tap a tile in the gap's row or column to slide the whole stack at once.
  • Sort each grid so it reads 1 upward with the gap last. Every tile you move costs one slip; run out of slips and the run ends.
  • The first time a tile lands on its home square it scores. Chain locking moves back-to-back to grow a combo multiplier up to ×5.
  • Clearing a grid banks your leftover slips as bonus points, and half of them carry into the next, bigger grid. Finish under par for extra.
  • N new run · H help · M sound · Enter restarts after a run ends.
