How to Play Checkers

Quick rules and site tips to get you moving.

Objective

Capture all of your opponent’s pieces, or leave them with no legal moves.

Basics

  • Red moves first. Pieces move diagonally on the dark squares.
  • Men move forward only; kings (with a crown) move both forward and backward.
  • Reach the far row to become a king.
  • Captures are jumps: hop over an adjacent opponent piece to an empty square.
  • Multiple jumps are allowed. If a capture is available, you must capture.

Using this site

  • Select & Move: Click a piece, then click a highlighted square.
  • Mandatory captures: Sources are outlined gold; landing squares glow green.
  • Multi-jump: If more captures are possible, the same piece stays selected until you finish.
  • Singleplayer: AI plays Black by default. Use “AI strength” to change depth.
  • Two-Player: Both humans play on the same device; switching modes restarts cleanly.
  • Undo: Reverts the last full turn (vs. AI it jumps back to your move).
  • New Game: Resets the board. Sound toggles SFX.

Shortcuts

  • H — Open Help
  • Esc or H — Close Help
