❓ How to Play Sky Hopper

Objective: Guide the bird through the pipes by tapping/clicking to make it flap. Avoid hitting the pipes or the ground!

Space or Click or Tap Make the bird flap upward

Gameplay:

  • Tap/click to make the bird flap and gain altitude
  • Navigate through the gaps between pipes
  • Each pipe you pass gives you 1 point
  • The game gets faster as you progress
  • Hit a pipe or the ground and it's game over!
