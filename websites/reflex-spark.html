Workshop Arcade

How to Play

Test your reflexes across five rounds. Tap the stage to start a round, then wait for it to flash green — tap as fast as you can.

  • Tapping during the red "Wait…" phase is a false start (it counts as a round but no time is recorded).
  • Best run is your lowest average across five rounds.
  • Keyboard: Space or Enter while the stage has focus.
  • Press Esc to close this dialog.
