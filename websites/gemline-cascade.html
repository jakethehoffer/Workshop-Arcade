How to play

Goal. Reach each level's target score before your moves run out. Every level banked grants bonus moves and a fresh hint.

Power gems. Match 4 to forge a Line gem — clearing it blasts its whole row and column. Match 5 or a cross to forge a Prism — clearing it shatters every gem of its colour. Blasts detonate other power gems.

Surge. Cascades and power gems charge the Surge bar. A full bar doubles all scoring for your next 6 swaps.

Controls. Tap two adjacent gems, or press a gem and swipe. Keyboard: ←↑↓→ move, Enter select and swap, H hint, M sound, R new run, F fullscreen.
