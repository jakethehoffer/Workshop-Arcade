🪜 Doodle Jump — Canvas Edition

Jump from platform to platform and climb as high as you can.
Land on special platforms for surprises.

Controls: A / ← left, D / → right, Space start & double-jump, P pause.

Platforms

Types unlock as you climb: Blue → Green → Red → Clouds → Rain clouds.
Blue = moving. Green = spring boost. Red = cracks — breaks on the 2nd bounce (normal bounce both times), explodes. Gray = cloud — softer bounce, resets double-jump, then fades. White-blue = rain cloud (moving) — soft bounce & resets double-jump, rains onto level: wet platforms reduce jump height briefly. A raindrop hitting you gives a tiny downward nudge.
New: moving green and red platforms behave like their static versions but slide left/right and speed up as you score more. Rain affects them the same as other non-cloud platforms.
