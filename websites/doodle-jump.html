Platforms

Blue • Moving Green • Spring boost Red • Cracks (breaks on 2nd bounce) Cloud • Soft bounce Rain Cloud • Soft bounce + rain Static • Normal
  • Blue platforms move horizontally.
  • Green platforms launch you higher.
  • Red platforms crack on first bounce and break on the second (normal bounce both times).
  • Clouds give a softer, lower bounce.
  • Rain clouds drift and spawn raindrops. Drops make platforms slippery and lightly nudge the player down.
  • Static platforms just sit there. Reliable.

Power-ups

🚀 Jetpack 👟 Jump Boots 🛡️ Shield ⏳ Slow-mo
  • Jetpack: rocket upward for a short duration.
  • Jump Boots: stronger bounces for a while.
  • Shield: one free rescue if you fall off-screen.
  • Slow-mo: world slows down (player physics unchanged).
