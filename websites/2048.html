❓ How to Play 2048

Objective: Combine numbered tiles to reach the 2048 tile! Keep playing to see how high you can go.

← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D Move all tiles in that direction
N Start a new game
Z Undo last move

How to Play:

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to slide all tiles in that direction
  • When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!
  • After each move, a new tile (2 or 4) appears randomly
  • Reach 2048 to win, but you can keep playing for a higher score
  • Game over when the board is full and no moves are possible
  • On mobile, swipe in any direction to move
