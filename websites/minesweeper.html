❓ How to Play Minesweeper

Objective: Reveal all tiles that don't contain mines without clicking on any mines!

Controls:

  • Left Click - Reveal a tile
  • Right Click - Flag/unflag a tile (mark as mine)
  • Flag Mode - Toggle to make touch controls easier (tap to flag, tap again to reveal)
  • N - Start a new game

How to Play:

  • Numbers show how many mines are adjacent to that tile
  • Use logic to figure out where mines are located
  • Flag tiles you think contain mines
  • Reveal all non-mine tiles to win!
  • Click a mine and it's game over
  • Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, or Custom difficulty
