How to Play

Return the ball with the left paddle. Longer rallies build pulse, brighten the table, and speed up the ball. First side to 7 wins.

  • Keyboard: W/S or ArrowUp/ArrowDown moves the paddle.
  • Touch: drag anywhere on the playfield to set the paddle height.
  • Space or Enter starts or restarts. F toggles fullscreen.
  • Esc closes this dialog or exits fullscreen.
