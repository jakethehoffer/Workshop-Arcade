❓ How to Play Arena

Objective: Survive as long as possible by avoiding enemies and collecting their dust particles!

Controls:

  • WASD, Arrow Keys, or Drag - Move your character
  • P or Esc - Pause / Resume
  • M - Toggle sound
  • Enter or Space - Start game / Restart

Gameplay:

  • Enemies spawn and move toward you - avoid them!
  • When enemies expire, they drop dust particles
  • Collect the dust particles to increase your score and advance to the next level
  • Dust particles also provide temporary invulnerability
  • Advancing to the next level clears the arena of enemies
  • The game is over when you are hit by an enemy
